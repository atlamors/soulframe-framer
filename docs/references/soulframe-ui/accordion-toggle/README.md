# Accordion toggle references

These screenshots preserve two Soulframe accordion/disclosure control treatments
for future vector tracing and component work.

## Source images

- `gold-emphasis-reference.png` — 122 × 82 crop with a gold-emphasis shell and
  ochre upward triangle.
- `neutral-reference.png` — 121 × 92 crop with a dark neutral shell and
  parchment-colored upward triangle.

## Shared visual traits

- Rectangular dark control face with no rounded corners.
- Thick, irregular black outer contour.
- Hand-drawn gold or muted stitched inset frame.
- Large centered triangular disclosure glyph with a dark outline.
- Adjacent circular medallion and ornament overlap the control shell.

## State caution

The screenshots do not establish whether the two treatments represent default,
hover, focus, active, expanded, or disabled states. Keep them named
`gold-emphasis` and `neutral` until the interaction mapping is confirmed.

The upward glyph may indicate an expanded accordion that will collapse when
activated, but that behavior is also intentionally unconfirmed.
