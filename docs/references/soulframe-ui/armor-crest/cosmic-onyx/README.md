# Cosmic Onyx Shield Concepts

Static material studies for the stat-sheet defense shield interior. These are design references, not production assets.

- `concept-a-restrained-violet-onyx.png` — dark violet onyx with a quiet center.
- `concept-b-plum-nebula-reliquary.png` — warmer plum and rose-gold nebula treatment.
- `concept-c-teal-violet-deep-field.png` — teal counterlight with violet celestial depth.
- `concept-d-celestial-eclipse-core.png` — broad-shouldered silhouette with embedded galactic geometry.
- `hybrid-v1-broad-crown-celestial-geometry.png` — A/C/D material hybrid with D's wide upper corners, B's moderate crown height, and more visible embedded astronomical geometry.
- `hybrid-v2-asymmetric-celestial-geometry.png` — preserves the hybrid material and silhouette while restoring D's sparse, off-center, perspective-warped astronomical fragments.
- `frame-concept-a-royal-astrolabe.png` — first composite frame study: blackened bronze, worn gold edges, celestial engraving, crown and shoulder anchors, and a reinforced lower point.
- `frame-concept-b-dirty-bronze-flowing-engraving.png` — thinner continuous aged-bronze frame with longitudinal celestial engraving that follows the shield perimeter.
- `reference-ornate-longitudinal-moldings.jpg` — user-supplied structural reference for nested raised rails, shadow channels, crown sweep, and lower convergence; botanical filigree is not part of the target direction.
- `frame-concept-c-sculptural-longitudinal-moldings.png` — ornate aged-bronze frame built from parallel sculptural moldings, with an integrated crown and crossed lower clasp.
- `frame-concept-d-restrained-royal-five-part-moldings.png` — restrained traditional-royal composite with a gripping bezel, paired shadow channels, dominant convex middle molding, narrow outer rail, calm structural sides, low integrated crown, and compact crossed lower clasp; celestial imagery remains confined to the approved onyx interior.
- `frame-concept-e-narrow-royal-moldings-chroma-source.png` — frame-only correction study on a flat magenta extraction background; narrows the dominant molding, removes the lower leaf finial, and keeps the center completely empty.
- `frame-concept-e-narrow-royal-moldings-frame-only-transparent.png` — alpha-extracted RGBA version of concept E for compositing independently over the approved gemstone/background layer.
- `frame-concept-f-structured-royal-ornamental-foundation-chroma-source.png` — structured ornamental frame study on a flat magenta extraction background, adding integrated crown collars, shoulder transitions, longitudinal leaf-tip relief bays, restrained beadwork, and a stepped lower ferrule.
- `frame-concept-f-structured-royal-ornamental-foundation-transparent.png` — alpha-extracted RGBA version of concept F for independent compositing over the approved celestial gemstone layer.
- `composite-concept-g-asymmetric-sage-bronze-vine.png` — full hierarchy study combining the approved celestial onyx, concept F's structured royal frame, and an asymmetrical external vine layer with soot-dark bronze stems, muted sage leaves, deliberate over/under weaving, a dominant left-side climb, and a small upper-right counterbalance.
- `frame-concept-h-faceted-royal-armature-transparent.png` — transparent RGBA frame-only study replacing concept F's bulbous tubular moldings and ring collars with planar forged straps, chamfered faces, stepped reveals, angular recessed panels, clean shoulder miters, and a compact geometric lower socket.
- `composite-concept-i-faceted-armature-sage-vine.png` — full hierarchy study combining the approved celestial onyx, concept H's faceted armature, and concept G's asymmetrical sage-bronze vine composition; tests the frame's stronger architectural geometry against the organic external silhouette.
- `frame-concept-j-narrow-body-ornamental-crown-chroma-source.png` — anatomy-focused frame study with very narrow faceted side and lower rails, gradual upper-shoulder expansion, concentrated nested crown moldings, and a tiny lower socket. The generated key background contains a magenta gradient; two local alpha-extraction attempts were rejected for haloing and bronze color shift, so no transparent derivative is archived yet.
- `composite-concept-k-narrow-crown-sage-vine.png` — refined hierarchy study combining the approved celestial onyx, concept J's narrow structural body and concentrated ornamental crown, and concept G's established asymmetrical sage-bronze filigree language refitted to the thinner rails.
- `composite-concept-l-frame-locked-regenerated-onyx.png` — clean base-fit study treating concept J's standalone frame proportions as the dimensional source of truth and regenerating the celestial onyx specifically for its taller crown-shaped opening; filigree is intentionally omitted for geometry review.
- `composite-concept-m1-filigree-left-ascent.png` — refined baseline placing the dominant sage-bronze filigree from the lower-left shoulder up most of the left rail, with a small upper-right echo and an open crown center.
- `composite-concept-m2-filigree-crown-drape.png` — corrected top-weighted study preserving Concept L's full-scale shield position while confining a compact upper-left crown/shoulder cluster to the upper half, balanced by a tiny isolated lower-right echo.
- `composite-concept-m2a-filigree-crown-drape-drifted.png` — preserved rejected first M2 attempt; its shield was visibly reduced and shifted upward, and its left drape extended below the midpoint into the intended calm lower half.
- `composite-concept-m3-filigree-lower-cradle.png` — bottom-weighted study forming an open lower-third cradle from the lower-left with one restrained left-edge ascent, leaving the crown and lower point readable.
- `composite-concept-n1-m1-lowres-160x180.png` — native stat-sheet-scale M1 readability study with fewer, larger leaf masses, thicker primary vine contours, wider negative spaces, simplified celestial noise, and stronger bronze/sage/onyx separation.
- `composite-concept-n2-m1-mobile-50x56.png` — smallest expanded-mobile-scale export of the same simplified M1 study, retained at its actual 50×56 display footprint for pixel-level legibility review.
- `composite-concept-n3-m1-balanced-lowres-160x180.png` — balanced native-size M1 study restoring sparse antique-gold astronomical fragments and a more characterful mid-density left ascent while retaining thicker primary contours, larger leaf masses, and open negative spaces for 160×180 readability.
- `composite-concept-n4-m1-icon-50x56.png` — dedicated 50×56 icon treatment derived from N3 rather than a plain shrink: three readable crown bands, a narrow high-contrast frame, one simplified left vine with five bold leaf masses and open curls, and a reduced violet/teal celestial field with only a few surviving stars and gold arc fragments.
- `composite-concept-n5-m1-faithful-icon-50x56.png` — fidelity-first 50×56 correction retaining N3's layered crown sweep, narrow faceted rails, full left-ascent leaf rhythm, upper-right echo, lower socket, and asymmetric celestial geometry; N4 contributes only stronger value separation and reduced microscopic texture, not redesigned anatomy.
- `composite-concept-n6-m1-larger-leaves-50x56.png` — leaf-focused N5 refinement retaining the faithful frame, vine route, curls, and celestial stone while merging adjacent small foliage into fewer leaf clusters, enlarging the surviving silhouettes, and removing fine serrations and vein noise for clearer native 50×56 recognition.
- `complete-simplified-v1-geometric-frame-large-leaves.png` — transparent full-resolution simplification study preserving the celestial onyx and M1-style left ascent while reducing the frame to narrow faceted rails, concentrating structure at the crown, and cutting the filigree to a few larger leaves with substantially more negative space.
- `complete-simplified-v1-geometric-frame-large-leaves-160x180.png` — native desktop-scale evaluation of the same simplified complete crest, retained at its actual 160×180 slot size to judge silhouette, leaf readability, and ornament density honestly.
- `complete-simplified-v2-larger-leaf-silhouettes.png` — targeted V1 refinement keeping the gemstone, frame, vine route, curls, and leaf count fixed while enlarging and broadening the existing sage-bronze leaves, simplifying their serrations, and strengthening their major vein folds.
- `complete-simplified-v2-larger-leaf-silhouettes-160x180.png` — native desktop-scale V2 evaluation confirming that the larger leaf masses survive downsampling without restoring the former filigree density.
- `complete-simplified-v3-side-mounted-rear-filigree.png` — structural layering revision moving all foliage behind the uninterrupted foreground frame, replacing the face-climbing vine with sparse lateral leaf sprays that widen the upper two-thirds into a rounder silhouette while retaining slightly greater weight on the left.
- `complete-simplified-v3-side-mounted-rear-filigree-160x180.png` — native desktop-scale V3 evaluation confirming the side leaves remain legible behind the border and broaden the crest without obscuring the gemstone or lower point.

The M1–M3 studies share Concept L's fixed shield geometry, narrow frame anatomy, celestial onyx opening, square crop, and near-black background; only the external filigree origin, flow, balance, and silhouette vary.

The first hybrid combines A's restraint, C's teal counterlight, and D's celestial geometry, using D's wide upper corners with B's moderate center crown.
